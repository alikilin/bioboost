## English

**BioBoost** does not collect or share your personal data with third parties.

The app uses the information you enter solely for the purpose of generating dating profile bios. This information is not stored on your device or on our servers after your session ends.

We use OpenAI's API to generate bio suggestions. Your input is sent to OpenAI for processing in accordance with [OpenAI's privacy policy](https://openai.com/privacy).

We do not collect names, email addresses, location data, or any other personally identifiable information.
