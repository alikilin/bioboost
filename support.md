# Support / Destek

## English

For any questions or issues with BioBoost, please contact us at:

**alikilin83@gmail.com**

We typically respond within 1-2 business days.

**Common questions:**
- **Refunds:** Contact Apple Support at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com)
- **Credits not showing:** Try closing and reopening the app, or use the "Restore Purchases" button on the paywall screen.
- **Bio not generating:** Check your internet connection and try again.
